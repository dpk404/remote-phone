RemotePhone lets you mirror and control your Android phone from your computer over WiFi. No USB debugging or ADB required.

Features:
* Screen mirroring at up to native resolution and 60 fps, with H.264 hardware encoding
* Full remote control: tap, drag, long press, scroll, system keys
* Live touch on Android 8+: a drag moves the phone as your hand moves
* Keyboard input into phone text fields, including the lock screen PIN pad
* Copy and paste between the phone and the computer
* Audio mirroring with automatic phone speaker muting (Android 10+)
* Approval on the phone before a computer can see or control the screen
* Encrypted connection
* Auto-discovery and auto-reconnect on the local network
* Wake on input: wakes the phone screen remotely

Clients:
* Desktop client for Linux, macOS and Windows: pip install remote-phone
* Browser client, nothing to install: https://dpk404.github.io/remote-phone/

Both devices must be on the same WiFi network. The app uses an Accessibility Service to dispatch touch gestures and text input from the computer.
